AOC-4 XBRL Generator

Create the MCA AOC-4 XBRL instance document (XML) for Non-Ind AS (C&I taxonomy) and Ind AS companies, check it against the MCA business rules, and download it. Your files are processed inside your browser and never uploaded.

1. Filing details

When you upload a filled template or an existing XML, its own company details are used for anything you leave blank here.

2. Upload your data

  • Filled InstantXBRL / TaxPrint template (.xls / .xlsx): every section and table is read.
  • Existing XBRL instance (.xml): to check or regenerate it.
  • Your own financial statements workbook (.xlsx): balance sheet, profit and loss and cash flow rows are matched to XBRL elements automatically; the scale (lakhs, thousands) is detected.
Starting Python in your browser (first visit downloads about 15 MB).

    Full desktop app

    The browser version covers import, validation and XML generation. The desktop app adds screens to type and edit every section and table, document upload into text blocks, saved filings and roll-forward to next year.

    1. Install Python 3.10 or newer from python.org.
    2. Download the app (zip) and extract it.
    3. Double-click run.bat. It opens in your browser at http://127.0.0.1:5050.
