How to prepare the filing

  1. Check company and period details.
  2. Upload the financial statements, directors' report, auditor's report and notes under Documents. Excel financial statements can be mapped to XBRL elements automatically; Word documents can fill text blocks.
  3. Fill or review each section in the left menu. Fields save automatically. Totals left blank are calculated from their components.
  4. Validate against the MCA business rules, fix errors, then generate the XML.
